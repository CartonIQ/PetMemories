# 🐾 PetMemories - Pet Diary & Age Tracker

**PetMemories** is a premium, 100% offline Android app designed for pet lovers. Track your pet's age, log their daily meals, save precious memories, manage vet expenses, and generate a professional "Pet Passport" or "Lost Pet Poster" in seconds.

## ✨ Features
- 🐶 **Multi-Pet Profiles:** Add unlimited pets with their photo, breed, microchip number, and weight.
- 🧮 **Smart Age Calculator:** Instantly calculates exact age (years/months) and next birthday countdown.
- 📸 **Memory Journal:** Save photos and notes of your favorite moments with your pet.
- 🍲 **Food Tracker:** Log daily meals, portions, and times to keep your pet healthy.
- 💰 **Expense Tracker:** Track vet bills, food, and toy expenses with multi-currency support (₹, $, £, €).
- 💊 **Vaccine Reminders:** Set local notifications for upcoming vaccinations and medications.
- 📄 **Pet Passport Generator:** Creates a stunning PDF/Image ID card with your pet's details and emergency contact.
- 🚨 **Lost Pet Poster:** Instantly generates a high-visibility poster with your phone number if your pet goes missing.
- ⚙️ **Backup & Restore:** Export all your pet data to a JSON file and restore it anytime.
- 🔒 **100% Offline & Private:** All photos and data are stored securely on your device. No servers, no tracking.

## 🚀 Installation
You can install PetMemories directly on your Android device.

1. Go to the **[Releases](https://github.com/CartonIQ/PetMemories/releases)** page.
2. Download the latest `PetMemories.apk` file.
3. Open the file on your phone and tap **Install** (you may need to allow "Install from unknown sources").

## 🛠️ Built With
- **Language:** Kotlin
- **UI:** Material 3, XML Layouts, ViewBinding
- **Architecture:** MVVM, Navigation Component
- **Local Database:** Room DB
- **Image Processing:** Android Canvas & FileProvider
- **Monetization:** Google AdMob (Banner, Interstitial, Native)
